Running Shoes: My Plan

1.   Architected a two-day, multi-stage running shoe evaluation covering sample procurement, size-matching, outdoor performance testing, and indoor lab teardown.
Result: Delivered a first-of-its-kind, editorially rigorous test model that allowed Verywell Fit to compare shoes by both real-world feel and internal construction — elevating the depth and authority of the final guide.


2. Coordinated all logistics for 15+ testers across scheduled time slots, mapping a structured outdoor course around Brookfield Place (grass, pavement, gravel) and a controlled treadmill session the following day.
Result: Captured consistent, comparable performance data across terrains and runner types, creating a dataset strong enough to support clear, trustworthy recommendations.


3. Hired and directed a specialized carpenter to saw each shoe in half and secured an on-site podiatrist to analyze internal layers, cushioning foams, stability frames, and outsole mechanics.
Result:   Produced rare, expert-verified insights connecting marketing claims to actual construction, which became a standout differentiator in the published feature and boosted its editorial credibility.
